01 · The problem

A production line can generate more observations than a quality team can inspect.

Manufacturing observations contain multiple operating conditions, while inspection time and investigation capacity are limited.

01

Too many observations

Product type, air temperature, process temperature, speed, torque and tool wear create a high-dimensional operating picture.

02

Limited inspection capacity

A quality engineer cannot treat every observation as equally urgent when only a limited number can be investigated.

03

Prediction alone is incomplete

A binary failure prediction does not by itself tell the team what to inspect first or which factors contributed to the risk.

the old question
“Will this observation fail?”
→
the better question
“Which observations should we inspect first — and why?”
02 · The solution

One workflow. Four jobs. A better decision loop.

PLQRA combines machine learning, statistics, visualization and inspection prioritization into a single quality-engineering experience.

01
ASSESS

Risk assessment

Classify production observations according to machine-failure risk.

02

03

Surface contributing factors through feature importance and SHAP-style explanations.

04

05 · Why it is different

The novelty is the decision layer.

PLQRA does not claim to be universally more accurate than existing predictive-maintenance models. Its difference is how it connects prediction to limited inspection capacity, explanations and interactive evidence.
